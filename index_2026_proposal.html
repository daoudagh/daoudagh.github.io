said.daoudagh@cnr.it

Software Engineering · Cybersecurity · Trustworthy AI · Human Movement Analysis · Digital Health

My research lies at the intersection of software engineering, cybersecurity, data protection and compliance, and AI-based human movement analysis. My long-standing work focuses on access and usage control, XACML-based policy testing, GDPR compliance by design, and the monitoring of distributed and complex systems. More recently, I have extended this research trajectory towards digital health and multimodal sensing, integrating human pose estimation, multi-person tracking, physiological signals, and requirements-driven software engineering to support movement analysis and the study of group dynamics in Dance Movement Therapy and active ageing.

Research

My work combines a long-standing software-engineering and cybersecurity line with a more recent research line on AI-enabled analysis of human movement and multimodal health data.

Software Engineering & Cybersecurity

Automated testing and validation of access- and usage-control systems; XACML policy engineering; mutation, combinatorial and model-based testing; continuous development and testing of security-critical systems.

Data Protection, Compliance & Trustworthy AI

GDPR compliance by design, translation of legal requirements into technical mechanisms, ontology-based monitoring, auditability, accountability, human oversight, and requirements-driven approaches for trustworthy AI-enabled systems.

AI-based Human Movement Analysis

Human pose estimation, multi-person tracking, identity preservation, participant-centred movement repositories, movement similarity, PCA-based characterisation, and network-oriented analysis of individual and group movement.

Digital Health & Multimodal Sensing

Integration of video-derived movement data with physiological signals and questionnaires, with current applications to Dance Movement Therapy, active ageing, and the analysis of group dynamics in naturalistic settings.

Software & Datasets

Selected research software and datasets developed or coordinated across my research lines.

DMT Identity Manager

Human-in-the-Loop environment for reviewing and correcting participant identities in multi-person tracking data. Supports identity fusion, frame transfer, frame swap, deletion, synchronized video inspection and structured audit logging.

DMTF: Tracking & Feature Extraction

Python-based toolchain for tracking people in video and extracting features for automated movement analysis in Dance Movement Therapy.

DMTF: PCA Analyzer

Framework for quantitative characterisation and comparison of individual and group movement patterns using missing-data handling, PCA, cosine similarity and Pearson correlation.

CMSD – Controlled Movement Similarity Dataset

Controlled experimental movement dataset organised into five similarity levels, each with 16 participants, designed to support validation of movement-analysis methods.

X-CREATE / XACMUT / XACMET / DOXAT

Research toolchain for XACML request generation, mutation testing, model-based testing, automated oracles and continuous testing of access-control systems.

GENERAL_D

Framework for deriving and testing GDPR-aligned access-control policies according to data protection by design.

MENTORS / MONTOLOGY

Ontology-based approach and supporting framework for monitoring System-of-Systems and complex distributed ecosystems.
